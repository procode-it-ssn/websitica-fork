About INVENTE

INVENTE, the flagship event of SSN College of Engineering and Shiv Nadar University Chennai, is an annual celebration featuring a diverse blend of technical and non-technical competitions.

Drawing participants from various colleges, the fest brings together coding challenges, robotics contests, hackathons, quizzes, debates, workshops, and fun games.

Beyond competition and learning, INVENTE fosters camaraderie, teamwork, and networking, creating an environment for students to learn, compete, and grow.

About SSN College of Engineering

SSN College of Engineering is a premier engineering institution founded in 1996 by Padma Bhushan awardee Mr. Shiv Nadar, with a strong focus on academic excellence, research, innovation, and holistic development.

Located in Chennai, SSN provides a vibrant environment where students are encouraged to learn, experiment, and build through research initiatives, innovation, and industry collaborations.

With a culture that nurtures talent and innovation, SSN empowers students to develop the knowledge and skills needed to shape the future.

About Shiv Nadar University Chennai

Shiv Nadar University Chennai is a premier multidisciplinary institution founded under the distinguished legacy of the Shiv Nadar Foundation, with a strong focus on academic excellence, research, innovation, and holistic development.

With an interdisciplinary curriculum spanning engineering, management, commerce, law, and humanities, SNU Chennai encourages students to think critically, collaborate, and solve real-world challenges.

Through experiential learning, research, and innovation, the university prepares students to become globally competent professionals and responsible citizens.
